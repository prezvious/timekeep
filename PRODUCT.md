# TimeKeep

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Product purpose

A browser-based stopwatch and countdown timer for measuring time and focusing on a task.

## Capabilities and constraints

- Stopwatch with pause, resume, reset, and lap recording.
- Countdown with hours, minutes, seconds, quick presets, pause, resume, reset, and a completion alert.
- Plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, served as a static website.
- Theme preferences persist on the device. Timing and lap data are local to the open page.
- Preserve the existing five optional themes: Matcha, Berry, Ocean, Sunset, and Lavender.

## Brand commitments

The name is TimeKeep. The user requested a modern, soft, focused workspace. The default must be neutral: no accent color until a user explicitly selects a color theme.

## Evidence on hand

The original index.html, style.css, script.js, README.md, and MIT LICENSE define the product. No marketing metrics or customer claims are supplied.
